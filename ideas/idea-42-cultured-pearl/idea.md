## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- Bead-cultured pearls: a bead nucleus plus a small piece of mantle tissue cut from a donor mollusk goes into the host's gonad; the epithelium cells multiply into a pearl sac around the bead, which deposits nacre. Tight contact between graft and bead yields a round pearl. [GIA Gems & Gemology, Winter 2017](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/winter-2017-akoya-cultured-pearl-farming-eastern-australia)
- Round nuclei are commonly made from US freshwater mussel shell. [GIA, Fall 2021](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/fall-2021-internal-structures-pinctada-maxima-pearls)
- Akoya pearls: 2-11 mm, most commonly 6-8 mm, cultured 10-24 months. The 2017 Australian akoyas were harvested after 18 months, in June-July; winter harvests give slower, tighter nacre. [GIA, pearl types](https://www.gia.edu/gia-news-research/differences-between-pearl-types), [GIA, Spring 2018](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/spring-2018-gemnews-very-small-akoya-cultured-pearls)
