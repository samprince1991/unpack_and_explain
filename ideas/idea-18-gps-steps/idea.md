## Caption

No internet, still a blue dot on your map. Your phone never sends a thing to a GPS satellite. It only listens.

About 30 satellites, roughly 20,000 km (12,500 mi) up, keep broadcasting where they are and the exact time. Your phone times how late each signal arrives. Multiply that delay by the speed of light and you get a distance. Four of those distances pin you down.

That's how GPS works, and why you get GPS without internet. The 4-step recap is on slide 8.
