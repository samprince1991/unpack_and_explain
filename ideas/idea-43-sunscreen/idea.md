## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10. Secondary sources (see verify).

- SPF 30 shields about 97% of UVB, SPF 50 about 98%; SPF measures UVB only; the difference is how much UV gets through, not how long it lasts; AAD recommends broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. [PBS NewsHour, 2026](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/do-you-need-spf-30-50-or-100-dermatologists-answer-your-burning-sunscreen-questions), [PolitiFact, May 2026](https://www.politifact.com/article/2026/may/22/do-i-need-spf-30-50-or-100-we-answer-your-burning/)
- Most people apply 20-50% of the amount used in testing. [OMRF](https://omrf.org/2023/07/25/bodywork-getting-to-the-heart-of-the-sunscreen-numbers-game)
