## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- In indigo denim the warp is dyed and the weft left white; in twill the warp passes over two weft threads, so the outside shows mostly dyed warp and the inside mostly white weft. [Wikipedia, Denim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denim)
- Indigo binds to the outside of the yarn ("ring dyeing"), leaving the core white; as it wears off the core shows, which is why denim fades. Rope dyeing gives the strongest ring effect. [Heddels](https://www.heddels.com/?p=1525), [Denimhunters](https://denimhunters.com/denim-wiki/denim-explained/why-denim-fades/)
