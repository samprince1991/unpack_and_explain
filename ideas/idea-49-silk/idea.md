## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- The cocoon filament is continuous, very long (up to 1,300 m) and extremely fine; usable length about 600-900 m. The larva is killed in the cocoon by steam or hot air, since an emerging moth would break the filament. Sericin is softened, the end found, and filaments from several cocoons are unwound together into one strand. Denier = grams per 9,000 m of filament. [Britannica, Sericulture](https://www.britannica.com/topic/sericulture)
