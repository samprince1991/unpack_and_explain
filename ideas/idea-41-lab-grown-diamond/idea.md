## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- Two methods: HPHT simulates the conditions of natural diamond growth; CVD grows diamond from a hydrocarbon gas mixture at moderate pressure and temperature in a vacuum chamber. Under deep-UV light, CVD diamonds reveal they grew layer by layer. [GIA](https://www.gia.edu/hpht-and-cvd-diamond-growth-processes)
- Most lab-grown diamonds passing through GIA's labs are CVD-grown, most with post-growth HPHT treatment to remove colour; spectroscopy tells how a diamond was grown. [GIA Gems & Gemology, Summer 2024](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/summer-2024-gia-update-on-laboratory-grown-diamonds)
- Lab-grown diamond is real diamond, chemically and crystallographically. [GIA 4Cs, laboratory-grown diamond](https://4cs.gia.edu/en-us/laboratory-grown-diamond/)
