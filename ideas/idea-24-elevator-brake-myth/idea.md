## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-06.

- Traction elevators must hang on at least 3 steel ropes (drum elevators 2) under ASME A17.1, as adopted in California's rules. [CA Title 8 §3042](https://www.dir.ca.gov/Title8/3042.html)
- The rope set must be rated for at least 7.6 times the load at the lowest speed, rising with speed (8.6 at 1 m/s, 200 ft/min). [Michigan R 408.41542, Table C](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/michigan/Mich-Admin-Code-R-408-41542)
- If the car goes too fast, the governor (a speed sensor in the machine room) first cuts the motor, then grabs the governor rope, which pulls the 'safeties': wedge brakes on the car that clamp the guide rails. [Otis](https://www.otis.com/tools-resources/high-rise-safety-systems)
- Elisha Otis showed his safety catch at New York's Crystal Palace in 1854: he had the only rope cut while he stood on the platform, and it dropped just a few inches. He sold his first safety elevator on 20 Sept 1853. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Elisha-Otis), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elisha_Otis)
- Correction: the first draft said the demonstration was in 1853; it was 1854.
