## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10. Secondary sources only (see verify).

- White gold is gold alloyed with whitening metals such as nickel, palladium or silver; the base often looks slightly yellow; a thin rhodium top layer makes it whiter and wears over time; palladium alloys need less plating. [Frank Jewelers](https://www.frankjewelers.com/blogs/blog/white-gold-and-rhodium-plating), [Gardens of the Sun](https://gardensofthesun.com/blogs/news/white-gold-turning-yellow)
