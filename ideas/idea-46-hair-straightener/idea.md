## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10. Secondary sources only (see verify).

- Heat loosens hydrogen bonds in keratin; once cooled they re-form in the new shape; humidity lets water re-break them; disulfide bonds aren't affected by humidity and need chemicals such as relaxers.
