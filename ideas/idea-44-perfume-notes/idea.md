## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- Notes are grouped by evaporation rate, which tracks molecular size: heavier molecules have more van der Waals contact, so need more energy to escape. Poucher's classic scale: evaporation coefficients 1-14 top, 15-60 middle, 61-100 base. [Cosmetics & Toiletries](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/formulating/function/fragrance/2695841.html)
- Heat speeds evaporation, so the opening fades sooner on warm skin. [Scento](https://www.scento.com/blog/how-perfume-notes-change-over-time.md) (secondary)
