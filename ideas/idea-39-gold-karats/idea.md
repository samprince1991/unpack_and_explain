## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- Fineness list: .375 = 9 ct (England, Canada), .417 = 10 ct, .583 = 14 ct (manufacturers commonly use 585), .750 = 18 ct, .833 = 20 ct; US legal minimum 10 ct; 9 ct is the lowest that may be called gold in the UK, France, Austria, Portugal and Ireland. [World Gold Council](https://www.gold.org/jewellery/technology/assay/index.html)
