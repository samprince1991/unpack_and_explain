## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- Gel contains photoinitiator, monomers and oligomers; the photoinitiator absorbs UV and fragments into free radicals that join monomers and oligomers into a growing polymer. [Dymax via Cosmetics & Toiletries](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/cosmetic-ingredients/article/21836591/dymax-corporation-what-are-oligomers-for-led-curable-nail-gel-coatings), [Dazzle Dry](https://dazzledry.com/blogs/journal/monomers-oligomers-and-photoinitiators-oh-my)
- Cured gels are very durable and don't chip as easily as lacquer; LED-curable gels cure faster than broad-spectrum UV ones. [Dymax](https://www.gcimagazine.com/home/article/21836591/dymax-corporation-what-are-oligomers-for-led-curable-nail-gel-coatings)
- Thinner layers cure more thoroughly (less light reaches the bottom of thick layers); oxygen can leave a tacky top layer. [Nailpro](https://nailpro.com/home/article/21156697/your-guide-to-nail-salon-product-ingredients), [US patent 8367045](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8367045)
