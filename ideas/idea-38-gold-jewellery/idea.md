## Verified facts

Checked 2026-11-10.

- 24 carat is pure gold, usually too soft for everyday jewellery; 18 carat = 75% gold + 25% other metals, often copper or silver. [World Gold Council](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/about-gold-jewellery)
- Fineness marks: .375 = 9 ct, .417 = 10 ct, .583 = 14 ct (commonly stamped 585), .750 = 18 ct, .833 = 20 ct. US legal minimum is 10 ct; UK, France, Ireland, Austria and Portugal allow 9 ct. [World Gold Council, assay](https://www.gold.org/jewellery/technology/assay/index.html)
- Lost-wax (investment) casting: a wax pattern (often injected into a rubber mould for copies) is encased in gypsum- or phosphate-bonded investment, burned out in a kiln (roughly 300 to 1350 °F / 150 to 730 °C ramp), then metal is forced in by centrifugal spin or vacuum. [Wikipedia, Lost-wax casting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost-wax_casting)
