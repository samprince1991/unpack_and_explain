## Caption

Both engines stopped at 41,000 feet. The crew still landed the plane.

In 1983, Air Canada Flight 143 ran out of fuel after a pounds-versus-kilograms mix-up. The pilots glided the jet to Gimli, Canada, which is why it's called the Gimli Glider.

Inside every jet engine: suck, squeeze, bang, blow.
